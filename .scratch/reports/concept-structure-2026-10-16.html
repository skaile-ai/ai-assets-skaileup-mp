1 The answer

_concept/ is one root folder in the project. It holds 14 first-level entries: three root files (brief.md, goals.md, glossary.md) and eleven numbered folders from 01_meta/ to 11_build/. 25 of 31 skills write into it. Each skill names its paths in metadata.artifacts.produces. 4 entries have more than one writer.

The numbers sort the folders for the reader. They do not give the write order: 10_blueprint/datamodel/ is written before 05_features/, and 09_mockup/ comes after the screens. Everything before 11_build/ is design. Everything in 11_build/ is build.

7 How one feature spreads across the treeRules and gaps

A feature owns files in 6 places. It shares 4 more files with other features. The feature spec is the center. Its <feature_slug> names the screen folder and the dossier. Frontmatter fields join the rest: story_refs, data_entities[], screens: and implements:. On the build side, the ledger names the spec in feature_ref, and 11_build/trace.yaml joins the feature to its slices and commits.
